Privacy Policy

How we protect your data and manage information across our service channels.

01

Third-Party Platform Use

When clients choose to work through Fiverr or Upwork, certain project communications, files, and payment records may be processed under those platforms' privacy policies. The Genius Agency may not control how those platforms manage user data.

Users are advised to review the following policies based on their chosen channel:

  • Fiverr Privacy Policy
  • Upwork Privacy Policy
02

Payment Data

Agency Direct

Payment data is handled through our integrated secure payment processor. We do not store sensitive financial information like full credit card numbers on our servers.

Fiverr / Upwork

Payment data is handled entirely by those platforms. The Genius Agency has no access to or control over your financial records on third-party marketplaces.

03

Communication Records

We maintain records of project-related communications to ensure service quality and project continuity. The storage location depends on your service channel:

  • Agency Direct: Stored securely within our internal encrypted systems.
  • Marketplaces: Stored and managed under Fiverr/Upwork's respective infrastructure.
04

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to coordinate projects and communicate with you — including your name, email address, and project details submitted via our contact or onboarding forms.

This information is used strictly for service delivery and internal administration. We never sell your personal data to third parties.
