Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services and how we work

How does payment work?

We use a secure escrow system. You pay upfront, but funds are only released to the expert after you approve the completed work.

Can I choose my own expert?

Absolutely! You can browse our expert directory and request a specific expert, or let us match you with the best fit.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times vary by project scope. Simple tasks may take 2-3 days, while complex projects can take weeks.

Can I request revisions?

Of course! We include revision rounds in every project until you're completely satisfied with the results.

Do you offer custom offers?

Yes! Every project is unique. We'll create a custom offer tailored to your specific needs and budget.

What if my expert is unavailable?

We'll immediately offer you equally qualified alternatives from our pool of vetted experts.
