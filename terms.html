Service Guidelines

Terms of Service

The governing framework for working with The Genius Agency across all platforms.

01

Service Model

The Genius Agency operates through a hybrid service model designed to provide flexibility, trust, and convenience for clients.

Clients may choose to work with us through any of the following channels:

Agency Direct Our primary platform
Fiverr Marketplace Expert
Upwork Marketplace Expert

The chosen service channel determines the payment structure, communication process, and dispute resolution framework for your specific project.

02

Payment Structure

For Agency Direct: Payments are securely processed through our approved payment partners. Projects officially begin after payment confirmation.

For Marketplaces (Fiverr/Upwork): Payments must follow the official payment systems of those platforms. The Genius Agency does not process or control payments made on third-party marketplaces.

03

Platform-Based Protection

Where Fiverr or Upwork is selected, the transaction is governed by the respective platform's policies, protections, dispute systems, and terms of service. Clients are responsible for reviewing those platform policies before beginning work.

04

Custom Offers

Custom Offers may be issued through The Genius Agency platform, Fiverr, or Upwork — depending on the selected service channel. All project scope, timelines, and pricing will be defined and agreed upon before execution.

05

Refunds & Disputes

  • Agency Direct: Refunds and disputes are reviewed and handled internally by The Genius Agency administration.
  • Fiverr/Upwork: All refund requests and disputes follow the marketplace's official resolution center protocols.
